ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

About Spaghetti Western Discovery

Spaghetti Western Discovery is a dedicated cultural archive and film discovery engine celebrating the golden era of European Western cinema (1961–1977).

"Between 1961 and the late 1970s, European filmmakers produced hundreds of gritty, stylish, and politically charged Westerns that forever reshaped world cinema."

The Cinematic Movement

From the iconic operatic trilogies of Sergio Leone to the gothic, mud-soaked imagery of Sergio Corbucci, the radical political allegories of Damiano Damiani and Sergio Sollima, and the acrobatic gadgetry of the Sartana and Sabata series, this project is designed to help fans and cinephiles unearth cult rarities and forgotten gems.

The 560+ Film Archive & Discovery Engine

Featuring a curated database of 560+ titles, our interactive randomizer lets enthusiasts explore directors, stars, music composers, and historical trivia one click at a time.

Each film entry includes alternative multinational release titles, full director & star credits, legendary composers (from Ennio Morricone to Bruno Nicolai and Gianni Ferrio), and concise 4–5 sentence editorial synopses.
